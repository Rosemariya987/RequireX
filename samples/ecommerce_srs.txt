# Software Requirement Specification (SRS)
## Project: GlobalMart Distributed E-Commerce Marketplace
## Standards Compliance: ISO/IEC/IEEE 29148:2018

### 1. Introduction & Purpose
GlobalMart is a high-volume omnichannel retail marketplace connecting buyers, vendor merchants, and logistics fulfillment partners.

### 2. Functional Requirements
* FR-001: The system shall enable registered consumers to browse product catalogs, filter by category/price, and perform full-text fuzzy searches.
* FR-002: The system shall manage persistent digital shopping carts with automatic price synchronization and inventory reservation locks.
* FR-003: The system shall process credit card, digital wallet, and UPI transactions via PCI-DSS certified payment gateways.
* FR-004: The system shall calculate localized sales taxes, international customs duties, and courier shipping rates dynamically during checkout.
* FR-005: The system shall notify customers via automated email and SMS upon order placement, shipment dispatch, and delivery.
* FR-006: The system shall allow merchant sellers to upload inventory spreadsheets, update SKU pricing, and manage return requests.
* FR-007: The application should be seamless and work efficiently across web and mobile browsers.

### 3. Non-Functional Requirements
* NFR-001: Scalability - The platform architecture shall auto-scale horizontally to support 25,000 requests per second during flash sale events without service disruption.
* NFR-002: Security - User passwords shall be hashed using Argon2id and API endpoints shall enforce OAuth 2.0 JWT bearer token authentication.
* NFR-003: Latency - The product catalog search endpoint shall return results in less than 150 milliseconds for 99% of requests.
* NFR-004: Availability - The checkout and order placement subsystems shall guarantee 99.99% high availability with zero single points of failure.
